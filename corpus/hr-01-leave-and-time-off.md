# Leave and Time Off Policy

## Annual leave
Every full-time employee receives 22 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.
Part-time employees receive annual leave in proportion to their contracted hours.
Annual leave of more than 3 consecutive days must be requested at least 10 working days in advance.
Your line manager approves annual leave in the PeopleHub system.

## Carry-over
You may carry over a maximum of 5 unused annual leave days into the next year.
Carried-over annual leave days must be used by 31 March or they are lost.
Unused annual leave is not paid out in cash, except when you leave the bank.

## Sick leave
Employees receive up to 12 days of paid sick leave per calendar year.
A medical certificate is required when sick leave lasts longer than 3 consecutive days.
Tell your line manager about sickness before 09:30 on the first day of absence.

## Parental leave
The primary caregiver receives 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave.
The secondary caregiver receives 4 weeks of fully paid parental leave.
Parental leave must start within 12 months of the birth or adoption.

## Bereavement and other leave
Bereavement leave is 5 paid days for the loss of an immediate family member.
Each employee may take 2 paid volunteering days per year with a registered charity.
Jury service is paid in full for its whole duration.
