# Service Desk and Equipment Guide

## Contact and hours
The Service Desk is open from 07:00 to 19:00 on weekdays.
Call the Service Desk on extension 4100 or open a ticket in the HelpNow portal.
Outside opening hours, only priority 1 incidents are handled by the on-call engineer.

## Response times
A priority 1 incident receives a first response within 15 minutes.
A priority 2 incident receives a first response within 4 hours.
A priority 3 request receives a first response within 2 business days.

## Equipment refresh
Laptops are replaced every 4 years.
Mobile phones are replaced every 3 years.
A damaged laptop is repaired or replaced within 3 business days.

## Remote access
The VPN must be used whenever you connect from outside a bank network.
Guest Wi-Fi is for visitors only and must not be used with bank laptops.
