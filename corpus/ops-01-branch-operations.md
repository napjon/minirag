# Branch Operations Manual

## Opening and closing
A branch must be opened and closed by 2 employees together, under dual control.
The vault requires 2 separate key holders to open.
The alarm must be set by the last 2 employees leaving the branch.

## Branch hours
Branches are open from 09:00 to 16:30 on weekdays.
Branches are open from 09:00 to 12:30 on Saturdays.
Branches are closed on Sundays and public holidays.

## Cash handling
The cash drawer limit for a teller is USD 5,000.
Cash above the drawer limit must be moved to the vault immediately.
A teller cash difference above USD 50 must be escalated to the branch manager on the same day.
Cash machines are replenished 2 times per week.

## Customer identification
A cash withdrawal above USD 10,000 requires 2 forms of customer identification.
A customer without valid identification cannot make an over-the-counter withdrawal.

## Complaints
A customer complaint must be acknowledged within 2 business days.
A customer complaint must be resolved within 15 business days.
Every complaint is recorded in the CareLog system on the day it is received.
