# Hybrid Working Policy

## Office attendance
Head office employees must work from the office at least 3 days per week.
Branch employees work on site for every shift because their roles serve customers in person.
Team anchor days are chosen by the department head and published in PeopleHub.

## Core hours
Core hours are 10:00 to 15:00, when every employee must be reachable.
Outside core hours you may arrange your working time with your line manager.

## Home office allowance
New hybrid employees receive a one-off home office allowance of USD 400.
Hybrid employees also receive a monthly connectivity allowance of USD 35.
The home office allowance is claimed through the expenses system with receipts.

## Working abroad
You may work from abroad for a maximum of 20 working days per calendar year.
A working abroad request must be approved at least 30 days before travel.
Working abroad is not allowed for roles with access to Restricted data.

## Equipment
Bank equipment must be returned within 7 days of your last working day.
Only bank-issued laptops may be used for hybrid work.
