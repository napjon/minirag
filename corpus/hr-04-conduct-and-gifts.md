# Code of Conduct and Gifts Policy

## Gifts and hospitality
You may accept a gift worth up to USD 75 from a client or supplier.
Gifts from a single source must not exceed USD 200 in total per year.
Every accepted gift must be recorded in the Gift Register within 5 working days.
Cash gifts and gift cards must never be accepted.

## Conflicts of interest
Every employee must submit a conflict of interest declaration by 31 January each year.
Outside employment requires written approval from Compliance before it starts.

## Personal trading
Personal trades in shares must be pre-cleared by Compliance.
Shares bought with pre-clearance must be held for at least 30 days.

## Speaking up
Concerns can be raised anonymously on the Speak Up line, 0800-555-0142.
The bank does not tolerate retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith.
