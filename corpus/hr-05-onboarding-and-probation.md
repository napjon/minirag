# Onboarding and Probation Guide

## First day
New employees receive their laptop and access badge on their first day.
Health cover starts on your first day of employment.
You are assigned an onboarding buddy for your first 90 days.

## Probation
The probation period is 6 months for all new employees.
Your first probation review takes place in week 12.
During probation the notice period is 2 weeks for either side.
After probation the notice period is 3 months.

## Pay and pension
Salaries are paid on the 25th day of each month.
The bank contributes 8% of your salary to the pension plan.
The minimum employee pension contribution is 4% of salary.

## Mandatory training
New employees must complete all mandatory training modules within 30 days of joining.
The mandatory modules are security awareness, conduct, data protection and fire safety.
