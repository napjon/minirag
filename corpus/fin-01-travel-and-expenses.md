# Travel and Expenses Policy

## Submitting a claim
Expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of the date of the expense.
A receipt is required for every expense above USD 25.
Approved expense claims are reimbursed within 10 working days.
Claims are submitted in the SpendDesk system and approved by your line manager.

## Meals
The meal limit is USD 60 per day for domestic travel.
The meal limit is USD 85 per day for international travel.
Alcohol is not reimbursed unless you are hosting a client.

## Hotels
The hotel limit is USD 180 per night.
In listed capital cities the hotel limit is USD 240 per night.
Hotels must be booked through the TravelPoint booking tool.

## Flights and rail
Flights shorter than 6 hours must be booked in economy class.
Business class is allowed for flights of 6 hours or longer with director approval.
Rail travel may be booked in first class when the journey is longer than 2 hours.

## Mileage and corporate cards
Private car mileage is reimbursed at USD 0.45 per kilometre.
Corporate card statements must be reconciled by the 5th day of each month.
A corporate card may never be used for personal purchases.
