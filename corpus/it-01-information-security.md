# Information Security Standard

## Passwords and sign-in
Passwords must be at least 14 characters long.
Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for every employee account.
Never share a password, including with the Service Desk.

## Devices
Your screen must lock automatically after 5 minutes without activity.
A lost or stolen device must be reported to the Service Desk within 1 hour.
USB storage devices are blocked on all bank laptops.
Software may only be installed from the Software Centre.

## Data classification
The bank uses 4 data classification levels: Public, Internal, Confidential and Restricted.
Restricted data must never be sent to an external email address.
Confidential data may be shared externally only through the SecureShare portal.
Customer account numbers are classified as Restricted data.

## Phishing
Report a suspicious email with the Report Phishing button in your mail client.
If you clicked a suspicious link, call the Service Desk on extension 4100 immediately.

## Security training
Security awareness training must be completed once every year.
New employees must complete security awareness training within 30 days of joining.
