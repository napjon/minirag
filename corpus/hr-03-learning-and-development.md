# Learning and Development Policy

## Learning budget
Each employee has an annual learning budget of USD 1,500.
Each employee may take 5 paid learning days per year.
Learning budget requests are approved by your line manager in PeopleHub.

## Professional certifications
The bank pays 100% of the exam fee for a first attempt at an approved certification.
The bank pays 50% of the exam fee for a second attempt.
You receive 3 days of paid study leave for each certification exam.

## Mentoring
The mentoring programme runs for 6 months and pairs you with a senior colleague.
Applications for the mentoring programme open in January and July.

## Repayment
If you leave within 12 months of completing a course that cost more than USD 3,000, you must repay the course fee.
